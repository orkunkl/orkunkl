I helped co-found Mozaik Studio, where I taught flow arts and yoga. I came to flow
arts through poi and staff spinning — a way to hold focus, work with stress, and
support my own practice. The studio ran for three years and has since closed.

The practice outlasted it. It taught me to steady my nervous system, move through
intense emotion with balance, and hold a safe space for other people.
