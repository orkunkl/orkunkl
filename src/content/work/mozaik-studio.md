I advised Mozaik Studio, where I taught flow arts and yoga. I came to flow
arts through poi and staff spinning — a way to hold focus, work with stress, and
support my own practice. I was involved for three years; the studio continues on
its own.

The practice outlasted my time there. It taught me to steady my nervous system, move
through intense emotion with balance, and hold a safe space for other people.
