Rosenberg's four components — observation, feeling, need, request — are simple enough
to look like a formula, which is what most people stop at. The thing that makes it
work is the split underneath: the same four components are used to express honestly
*and* to receive empathically, listening for what the other person is observing,
feeling, needing, and requesting even when their words arrive as blame.

The load-bearing move is the third one. Needs are universal — safety, rest,
connection, autonomy, meaning — and nobody has a need for a specific person to do a
specific thing. What arrives as a demand is always a strategy standing in for a need.
