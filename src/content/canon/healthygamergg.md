Dr. K nearly failed out of college on video game addiction, went to India, and spent
seven years studying to become a monk before coming back to do an MD and MPH at Tufts
and a psychiatry residency at Mass General/McLean. Both halves are in the channel:
evidence-based contemplative practice and hard clinical psychiatry, pointed at the
thing the internet is doing to attention.

The channel offers a working model rather than inspiration — how motivation actually
behaves, what avoidance is doing underneath, why willpower fails and what to do instead.
He treats the relationship with technology as a clinical problem rather than a moral one.
