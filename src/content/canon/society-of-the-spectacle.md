Debord's 1967 book-length thesis, written in numbered theses like a manifesto. The
spectacle is not the mass media. It is a social relation mediated by images — the
point at which capital has accumulated so far that it becomes an image.

It came thirteen years before Baudrillard's *Simulacra and Simulation*, and
Baudrillard built on it. Where Debord still has a real world being veiled by
appearances, Baudrillard drops the world behind the veil altogether. Reading them in
order is watching that shift happen.
