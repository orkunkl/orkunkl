Kufayev restores Kuṇḍalinī to the centre of the Trika Śaiva vision: not an esoteric
force, not a psychological metaphor, but the self-revealing power of consciousness
whose vibration gives rise to the world. It comes out of decades of teaching, not
reading — the book is drawn from live sessions with a shaktipat community.

It maps the whole arc: slumber to awakening, awakening to integration, integration
to embodiment. The argument that matters most is the last one — spiritual life is
not an escape from form. It is the transfiguration of form itself.

First in a trilogy, after *Camatkāra: The Hidden Path*.
