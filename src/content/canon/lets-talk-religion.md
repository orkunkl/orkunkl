Filip Holm, a Stockholm-based public educator with a master's in the academic study of
religions, making video and podcast lectures on religion, history and philosophy —
from the major traditions down to small, misrepresented schools and individual
mystics. Rigorous without being dry.

The channel's real gift is method. It keeps insisting that religions are internally
diverse and change over time and place; that applying a Christian template — founder,
scripture, creed — to Hinduism or Islam produces confident nonsense; that the border
between "religion" and "culture" is a modern invention. That habit of asking who is
defining the category, and in whose interest, is the channel's method in one line.
