Kṣemarāja's short, dense summary of Pratyabhijñā — the philosophy of recognition.
Twenty sutras, each one an arrow. The claim underneath them: nothing needs to be
added to you and nothing needs to be removed. What is missing is only the seeing.

Wallis's *The Recognition Sutras* carries the Sanskrit alongside a commentary that makes
the argument legible without flattening it.

**Also translated by:** Jaideva Singh, *Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam: The Secret of Self-Recognition*.
