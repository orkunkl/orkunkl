Wilson's guidebook, grown out of a 1979 doctoral thesis. It lays out Timothy Leary's
eight-circuit model of consciousness — four "antique" circuits everyone has, four
later ones that he argues exist only in minorities so far — and pairs each with
exercises. Korzybski's general semantics and Crowley's Thelema run underneath.

The exercises are the point. You don't read this book so much as run it on yourself:
the map is not the territory, and Wilson wants you to notice the difference in your
own head rather than agree with him about it.

> We are all giants, raised by pygmies, who have learned to walk with a perpetual
> mental crouch.
