Dyczkowski's study of the doctrine and practices of the non-dual tradition, built around a
single claim: spanda — the vibratory dynamism of absolute consciousness — is the
cardinal principle, not a side doctrine. He traces how the Spanda school relates to
the other schools of the tradition and where it parts from them.

The companion volume, *The Stanzas on Vibration*, gives the Spanda Kārikā alongside
four commentaries — Kṣemarāja's, Kallaṭabhaṭṭa's, Rājānaka Rāma's, and
Bhagavadutpala's — so the text and its reading arrive together.
