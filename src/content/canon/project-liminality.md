Project Liminality is David Rug's attempt to build the missing layer between two things
everyone agrees are broken: how we think together, and how we coordinate. He studied
physics in Heidelberg, ran into the GameB conversation, and came away with what he
calls a *physics of communication* — the claim that sense-making and coordination are
not soft problems but have structure you can design against.

The channel's central work is **Immanent Metaphysics**, the formal system Forrest
Landry developed through the 1990s. Its companion video,
[**The Age of Miracles**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yrgSwgqclJU), carries the whole
argument in one arc — from Landry's system, down through Moloch, and back out to the more
beautiful world.

Its compact statement is *love is that which enables choice; fear is what consumes it*.
From that single distinction Rug derives two modes of mind: **domesticated**, which uses
story as a tool — narrative — and **liminal**, which serves story as a being — vision.
Domesticated mind cannot feel that its choices are entangled with everyone else's, so the
behaviour of others looks like chance to it. From there the step to "winners and losers,
predator and prey" is small, and from there to game theory is automatic. Rug's
observation is that Nash's framework is not wrong, it is *local*: he calls it the physics
of fear, and it describes exactly the slice of the world where fear has already consumed
choice.

The multipolar trap is not a failure of intelligence — it is what intelligence looks like
once the sense of connection is gone.

Then the **third attractor**, which is where the series lands. The frame comes from
Daniel Schmachtenberger's *In Search of the Third Attractor*: if the first attractor is
civilisational breakdown and the second is the control dystopia built in response, the
third is neither. Project Liminality's own pass at it is in
[**The InterBrain**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v5dBRmjF8mc), which opens on
exactly that chapter. What it adds is specificity: stewarded technology, sense-making
worth the name, and a coordination layer that makes the connection between people
*visible* rather than merely asserted. That last part is the software the video is named
for — a knowledge-gardening system that organises along your actual relationships instead
of hierarchies, with Dream Nodes implemented as git repositories and a Coherence Beacon
that surfaces links you did not know you had.
