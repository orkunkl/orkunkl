Inayat Khan was an Indian Sufi master of the Chishti order — the first teacher to
bring Islamic mysticism to the West — and an accomplished musician who toured
Indian classical music through Europe and America. Both halves are in this book.

His claim is that music is not an art that gestures at the divine. It is a small
instance of the harmony the universe is already made of, which is why it moves us
the way it does. The chapters range from the mysticism of sound proper through
language, breath, and the music of the spheres.
